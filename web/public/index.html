What this is

The real game, not an emulator

This is Platinum itself, running natively on your machine with online play built right in. Maps, cutscenes, battles and music are the game's own. It looks and plays like Platinum because it is Platinum.

Your save travels with you

Your party, your boxes, your bag, your money and your story progress all live safely on the server, not on one device. Sign in from your PC or your phone and pick up exactly where you left off.

Bring your own game

Nothing you download here contains Nintendo's ROMs, art or sound. The client needs legal backups of three cartridges you own: Platinum, which it runs on, and HeartGold and Black, which it reads its extras out of. SoulSilver and White stand in for those two, and Diamond or Pearl can lend their art on top.

What's in the game

A shared overworld

Other players walk the same maps you do, with names over their heads and their follower Pokémon beside them. One continuous, persistent world.

Chat

Local, global and trade channels, whispers by name, and a battle channel while you fight.

Trading and a global market

Trade face to face, or list Pokémon and items on the global trade market: search, filters, price history, payouts held until you collect.

PvP duels

Challenge another player on the spot. The fight is run by the engine's own link-battle code, so it plays exactly like the real thing.

Guilds, friends, parties

Guilds with ranks, permissions and a message of the day. Friends and block lists. Link up with up to three others.

The single-player game, kept

Wild encounters, catching, trainer battles that notice you coming, badges, the Pokédex, PC boxes, HMs, the Poketch, all on the cartridge's own scripts.

Multiplayer where the DS only pretended

Meet someone in the Underground and the game's own conversations play out between you. Super Contests seat two to four real players, and the Battle Frontier's link facilities take a real partner instead of the wireless the cartridge wanted.

Mail

A persistent mailbox with inbox and sent folders, so a message keeps until its reader signs in.

A native client

Windows, Linux and Android builds. HD mode, 60 fps, gamepad support, key rebinding, signed automatic updates.
