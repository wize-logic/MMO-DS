What this is

The real engine, not an emulator

The client is the game's own engine, compiled natively for your machine with the online code fused in. Maps, scripts, cutscenes, battles and music are the game's own, so it looks and plays exactly as it always did.

The server owns the world

A login server, a game server, and a network protocol between them. The server owns everything: your party, your boxes, your bag, your money, your story progress. The client displays the world; the record lives on the server, so your save is wherever you sign in.

Bring your own game

Nothing you download here contains a ROM, art or sound. The client needs a game image you build yourself. Other cartridges you own can lend their extras, their art and their soundtracks.

What's in the game

A shared overworld

Other players walk the same maps you do, with names over their heads and their follower monsters beside them. One continuous, persistent world.

Chat

Local, global and trade channels, whispers by name, and a battle channel while you fight.

Trading and a global market

Trade face to face, or list monsters and items on the global trade market: search, filters, price history, payouts held until you collect.

PvP duels

Challenge another player on the spot. The fight is run by the engine's own link-battle code, so it plays the way a link battle always did.

Guilds, friends, parties

Guilds with ranks, permissions and a message of the day. Friends and block lists. Link up with up to three others.

The single-player game, kept

Wild encounters, catching, trainer battles that notice you coming, badges, the Pokédex, PC boxes, HMs, the Poketch, all on the cartridge's own scripts.

Multiplayer where the DS only pretended

Meet someone in the Underground and the game's own conversations play out between you. Super Contests seat two to four real players.

Mail

A persistent mailbox with inbox and sent folders, so a message keeps until its reader signs in.

A native client

Windows, Linux and Android builds. HD mode, 60 fps, gamepad support, key rebinding, signed automatic updates.
